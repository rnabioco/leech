**Label-divergence guard for `data prepare` and the pipeline.** `--label-divergence-position P` (0-based from the first base of `--motif`) declares where the label-defining sequence starts; leech derives the focus-relative `D = P - --motif-offset` and refuses, before any read is opened, a window in which any stored input (features, k-mer feature channels, raw signal, signal residual, sequence) can reach `+D`, unless `--allow-window-past-divergence` is given. The verdict is recorded in `prepare_config.json`, carried through `data merge` (which refuses disagreeing or half-declared inputs) into the model's `config.json` and `eval test`'s `metrics["leakage"]`. The pipeline requires `label_divergence_position` (null accepted), checks it at DAG build, forwards it from all four `data prepare` rules, and surfaces `leakage_status` in its summary tables.
