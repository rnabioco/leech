**`--dwell-template-table`'s position coverage of the training/inference feature window is now checked, logged and recorded.** A template table covers a fixed `position` range, and every feature column outside it silently got a constant ratio of 1.0 -- a `--feature-end` wider than the table trained some channels that were constant over part of their width, invisibly. `LeechDataset` and bundle inference now log the table's range against the resolved feature window at INFO, warn when some columns fall outside it, and raise `ValueError` when there is no overlap at all. `load_dwell_template_table` returns a `DwellTemplate` dataclass (replacing the 5-tuple from #365) that also carries every `# key: value` header line the table has, verbatim; `config.json` now records `dwell_template_positions`, `dwell_template_uncovered_columns` and `dwell_template_header` alongside #365's `dwell_template_motif_offset`/`dwell_template_motif`.
