**Bundle architecture configs no longer leak training-only fields.** `_TRAINING_PARAMS` (the denylist `_architecture_config` uses to strip training hyperparameters out of a bundle's stored config) was missing about 15 keys `config.json` now writes — `label_smoothing`, the newer augmentation and adversarial/CL-regression knobs, `checkpoint_metric`, `gpus`, and others — so those leaked into pairwise-model architecture comparisons and the config a bundle ships. `tests/test_training_advanced.py` now imports the real `_TRAINING_PARAMS`/`_architecture_config` instead of re-declaring a stale copy inline.
