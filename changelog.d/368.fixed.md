**`--augment-time-stretch` and `--dwell-template-table` now refuse to combine.** Time-stretch rescales the `dwell` feature channel per sample, but the dwell-template ratio channels are computed once at construction and were never rescaled to match, so under stretch they silently stopped equalling `dwell / expected`. `LeechDataset` now raises `ValueError` at construction when both are set, the same shape as the existing `shift_max_bases`/`time_mask_bases` refusal.
